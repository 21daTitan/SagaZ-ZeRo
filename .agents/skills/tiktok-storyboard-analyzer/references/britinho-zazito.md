# Britinho/Zazito Adaptation Rules

Use these notes only when the user asks to adapt, compare viral potential, or model references for Britinho/Zazito.

## What To Preserve

- Preserve the reference video's **mechanic**, not its exact IP or character skin.
- Translate global brainrot into Brazilian social conflict when useful.
- Keep the first 2 seconds visually obvious: humiliation, body/material transformation, money/status, family betrayal, chase, or impossible object.

## Britinho Fit Criteria

Strong fits:

- family poverty vs elite/rich setting;
- adopted outsider, favorite sibling, rival, or intruder;
- status theft, fake richness, inheritance, school, mansion, pedreira, or public humiliation;
- physical object logic: bag, cart, safe, car, furnace, concrete mixer, grill, sack, cage, wallet, trophy;
- payoff where someone becomes material: stone, powder, coal, concrete, guacamole-like goo, smoke, dust, fake jewelry, sack of gravel;
- second payoff where parents or NPCs casually use the punished character as an object.

Weak fits:

- pure celebrity/IP dependence;
- clean moral drama without grotesque payoff;
- too many unclear morphs or untrackable object changes;
- humor that only works in English wordplay.

## Recommended Adaptation Formula

1. Ordinary poor-world setup.
2. Outsider or object threatens Britinho's ego/status.
3. Britinho has one silent jealousy beat.
4. Fake friendship or fake innocence.
5. Clear physical sabotage/exploitation.
6. Money/status/aura spike.
7. Public flex or humiliation of someone else.
8. Reveal/turn.
9. Cartoony material punishment.
10. Second payoff with a dry Brazilian line.

## Dialogue Rules

- Keep lines short and dublable.
- Use direct Brazilian phrasing, not corporate/guru language.
- Every line should serve envy, humiliation, threat, fake kindness, panic, superiority, or punishment.
- Britinho can be cruel, but violence should stay material/cartoon, not realistic gore.
